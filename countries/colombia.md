# Colombia

> Last reviewed: 2026-10-22.
> Access notes: public data may be split across national agencies, chambers of commerce and local authorities; confirm the record type and date.

#### Digital Landscape

Colombia has a comparatively accessible public-information ecosystem combining national portals, chambers of commerce, open contracting data and Spanish-language investigative reporting. Entity names, NITs, municipalities and contract identifiers are important disambiguation keys.

#### Intelligence Agency & OSINT Tradecraft

- Publicly documented institutions include the National Intelligence Directorate (DNI), Fiscalía General de la Nación, Policía Nacional and the Procuraduría General de la Nación.
- Do not treat media or social-media allegations as findings. Distinguish investigation, indictment, administrative sanction and final judgment.

#### Country-Specific OSINT Tools

- RUES — https://www.rues.org.co — business and entity searches across Colombian registry categories.
- SECOP services — https://www.colombiacompra.gov.co/secop/secop-ii — public-contracting platforms and guidance.
- OpenSanctions — https://www.opensanctions.org/datasets/ — cross-border sanctions and PEP lead generation; verify against Colombian primary records.

#### Ultimate Beneficial Ownership (UBO) Sources

Use RUES, Superintendencia de Sociedades filings and official corporate documents as starting points. Do not treat a basic RUES search as a complete UBO register. Ownership inferences require corroboration and an explicit confidence label.

#### Public Procurement & Tendering

- Colombia Compra Eficiente — https://www.colombiacompra.gov.co — official gateway to SECOP I, SECOP II, TVEC and procurement-data services.
- Preserve the contract identifier, entity, supplier, stage, amount, amendments and publication date when building a procurement chronology.

#### Notable Cases

For investigations involving public contracts or corporate networks, combine SECOP records with RUES, regulator publications, court documents and independent reporting. Record the exact status of each allegation or proceeding.

#### Legal Considerations

- The SIC is Colombia's official authority for personal-data protection and provides guidance and services through its site.
- Public availability does not eliminate privacy, defamation or access restrictions. Avoid bulk collection of personal information unrelated to a defined lawful purpose.
- This section is a research guide, not legal advice.
