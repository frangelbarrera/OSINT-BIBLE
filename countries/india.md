# India

> Last reviewed: 2026-10-22.
> Access notes: portal availability, login requirements and regional restrictions can change; confirm the current access path before relying on a record.

#### Digital Landscape

India has a large, multilingual digital environment. English and Hindi are widely used in national research, while state-level work may require regional-language sources. Treat platform content, identity records and leaked-data claims as leads that require corroboration.

#### Intelligence Agency & OSINT Tradecraft

- Publicly documented national-security and law-enforcement institutions include the Intelligence Bureau, Research and Analysis Wing, National Investigation Agency and state police forces. Their operational methods and internal OSINT capabilities are not treated as public facts here unless supported by an official publication.
- For open-source work, distinguish official records from media reporting and user-generated material. Do not infer identity from a name match alone.

#### Country-Specific OSINT Tools

- Indian Kanoon — https://indiankanoon.org — searchable Indian case-law resource; verify the underlying court and date.
- OpenCorporates — https://opencorporates.com — cross-jurisdictional company lead generation, not a substitute for MCA records.
- OpenSanctions — https://www.opensanctions.org/datasets/ — sanctions and politically exposed person datasets; check dataset provenance and update time.

#### Ultimate Beneficial Ownership (UBO) Sources

India's public corporate information is distributed across MCA services and other regulatory disclosures. Do not describe MCA master data as a complete public beneficial-ownership register. Use MCA filings and official regulatory records where available, and corroborate ownership in multiple documents.

#### Public Procurement & Tendering

- Central Public Procurement Portal — https://eprocure.gov.in — search tenders, corrigenda, awards and procurement documents.
- Government e-Marketplace references are accessible through the CPPP ecosystem; verify the current portal path before citing a specific bid record.

#### Notable Cases

Use court orders, parliamentary records, regulator orders and official investigation publications as the primary evidence chain. Avoid adding case narratives here without a dated primary source and an independent corroborating source.

#### Legal Considerations

- India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and related rules or notifications should be checked against current MeitY publications before collecting or publishing personal data.
- Public availability does not remove privacy, defamation, copyright or access restrictions. Do not attempt to bypass authentication, rate limits or portal controls.
- This section is a research guide, not legal advice.
