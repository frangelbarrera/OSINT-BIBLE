# Indonesia

> Last reviewed: 2026-10-22.
> Access notes: several official services are primarily in Indonesian and may require an account, payment or a local workflow.

#### Digital Landscape

Indonesia's OSINT environment is multilingual and geographically distributed across national and provincial institutions. Indonesian-language sources are essential for official records and local reporting. Verify an entity's legal form, province and registration authority before drawing conclusions.

#### Intelligence Agency & OSINT Tradecraft

- Publicly documented institutions include the State Intelligence Agency (BIN), Indonesian National Police and Corruption Eradication Commission (KPK).
- Do not infer undisclosed collection methods. Separate official statements, court decisions, investigative reporting and user-generated claims.

#### Local Sources & Press

- Tirto.id — https://tirto.id
- Hukumonline — https://www.hukumonline.com
- Indonesia Corruption Watch — https://antikorupsi.org

Use local reporting as a lead and corroborate important claims with AHU, OJK, court, procurement or KPK records.

#### Country-Specific OSINT Tools

- AHU legal-entity services — https://ahu.go.id — official starting point for Indonesian entity research.
- BPS WebAPI and data services — https://webapi.bps.go.id/developer — official statistical access documentation.
- OpenSanctions — https://www.opensanctions.org/datasets/ — cross-border sanctions and PEP lead generation; verify against primary records.

#### Ultimate Beneficial Ownership (UBO) Sources

AHU links to an official beneficial-owner service. Confirm whether a specific record is public, requires authentication or has a fee before describing it as openly searchable. Cross-check ownership with IDX filings, OJK publications and corporate documents.

#### Public Procurement & Tendering

- LKPP — https://www.lkpp.go.id — official procurement institution and policy source.
- For a specific tender, identify the relevant procurement system and contracting authority; do not assume the national policy portal is itself the complete tender database.

#### Notable Cases

For corporate, corruption or procurement research, preserve the Indonesian entity name, legal form, registration identifier, contracting authority and document date. Mark allegations, investigations, charges and judgments separately.

#### Legal Considerations

- Indonesia's Personal Data Protection Law No. 27 of 2022 should be checked against current official guidance before processing personal data.
- Indonesian-language records may have different publication and access conditions from English summaries.
- This section is a research guide, not legal advice.
