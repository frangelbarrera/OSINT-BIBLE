# South Africa

> Last reviewed: 2026-10-22.
> Access notes: some company disclosures and procurement records may require an account, payment or a separate government service.

#### Digital Landscape

South Africa has a multilingual media and public-sector information environment. National research often uses English-language portals, but provincial and community sources can add material context. Verify dates, province and entity identifiers carefully because many records are held at different government levels.

#### Intelligence Agency & OSINT Tradecraft

- Publicly documented security institutions include the State Security Agency, South African Police Service and Directorate for Priority Crime Investigation (the Hawks).
- Do not infer undisclosed intelligence capabilities from job postings, vendor claims or social-media content. Separate official allegations, investigative reporting and court findings.

#### Country-Specific OSINT Tools

- CIPC search and disclosure services — https://www.cipc.co.za/?page_id=1649 — corporate search route linked by the official commission.
- National Treasury eTenders — https://www.etenders.gov.za — tender opportunities and procurement data.
- OpenSanctions — https://www.opensanctions.org/datasets/ — cross-border sanctions and PEP lead generation; verify against primary records.

#### Ultimate Beneficial Ownership (UBO) Sources

CIPC provides corporate and disclosure services, including beneficial-ownership-related material through its current service structure. Confirm the current disclosure type and access conditions; do not describe the portal as a universally open UBO database.

#### Public Procurement & Tendering

- National Treasury eTenders — https://www.etenders.gov.za — search by tender number, organ of state and province, and browse procurement information.
- The portal states that it covers tenders from public-sector organisations across government spheres. Confirm whether an individual record is active, closed, amended or cancelled.

#### Notable Cases

For corporate or procurement investigations, preserve the tender number, entity identifier, publication date, amendments and award record. Use court or regulator documents to distinguish allegations from findings.

#### Legal Considerations

- POPIA and PAIA are administered by the Information Regulator and affect personal-data handling and access-to-information work.
- Public records may still contain personal information subject to lawful-processing and publication limits.
- This section is a research guide, not legal advice.
