# United Arab Emirates

> Last reviewed: 2026-10-22.
> Access notes: the UAE has federal, emirate-level and free-zone systems; a single federal public company search should not be assumed.

#### Digital Landscape

The UAE's public information environment is distributed across federal government services, emirate authorities and financial free zones. Research should record the emirate, free zone and licensing authority for each entity. English and Arabic sources may contain different levels of detail.

#### Intelligence Agency & OSINT Tradecraft

- Publicly documented security institutions include the State Security Department, the Federal Intelligence and Investigation Authority and police forces at emirate level.
- This guide does not infer undisclosed intelligence capabilities. Treat official statements, commercial intelligence claims and media reporting as distinct evidence types.

#### Country-Specific OSINT Tools

- DIFC public information and registry services — https://www.difc.ae
- ADGM public registers and legal resources — https://www.adgm.com
- OpenSanctions — https://www.opensanctions.org/datasets/ — cross-border sanctions and PEP lead generation; verify against the relevant authority.

#### Ultimate Beneficial Ownership (UBO) Sources

The UAE does not provide one simple public federal UBO search covering every emirate and free zone. Use the relevant licensing authority and public corporate disclosures where available. Treat ownership inferred from commercial databases as a lead, not as an official finding.

#### Public Procurement & Tendering

Procurement is distributed across federal and emirate-level government entities. Start from the UAE Government portal and the relevant authority; do not present a third-party tender aggregator as a national official register without confirming its provenance.

#### Notable Cases

Record the emirate, court or free-zone authority and document date for any corporate or regulatory matter. UAE federal, emirate and free-zone legal systems should not be collapsed into one undifferentiated source category.

#### Legal Considerations

- The UAE Government portal identifies Federal Decree Law No. 45 of 2021 on the Protection of Personal Data and explains rights, processing controls and cross-border-transfer provisions.
- DIFC and other free zones may have their own data-protection and regulatory frameworks.
- The UAE's cybercrime and internet-access rules can affect publication and access. This section is a research guide, not legal advice.
