# Nigeria

> Last reviewed: 2026-10-22.
> Access notes: public portals may require registration, payment or a browser session; confirm current access before relying on a record.

#### Digital Landscape

Nigeria's research environment combines federal portals, state-level records, English-language reporting and rapidly changing online platforms. A company or person should not be identified from a single name, phone number or social profile; use registration identifiers, dates and independent corroboration.

#### Intelligence Agency & OSINT Tradecraft

- Publicly documented security and investigative bodies include the Department of State Services, Nigeria Police Force, National Intelligence Agency and Economic and Financial Crimes Commission.
- This guide does not infer undisclosed collection methods or operational capabilities. Treat official statements as one source and corroborate material claims independently.

#### Country-Specific OSINT Tools

- CAC public search — https://icrp.cac.gov.ng/public-search — business-name and company verification route linked by the official CAC site.
- OpenCorporates — https://opencorporates.com — cross-border company lead generation; verify against CAC.
- OpenSanctions — https://www.opensanctions.org/datasets/ — sanctions and PEP datasets; review source provenance and update time.

#### Ultimate Beneficial Ownership (UBO) Sources

Use CAC records and official filings as the starting point. Do not describe the public CAC search as a complete beneficial-ownership register. Where ownership is not directly disclosed, mark it as an inference and corroborate it with contracts, regulatory records or court documents.

#### Public Procurement & Tendering

No national procurement link is included as verified in this pass. The Bureau of Public Procurement should be checked directly before adding a portal or tender source; do not substitute an unofficial aggregator.

#### Notable Cases

For investigations involving corruption, corporate ownership or public contracts, build the chronology from CAC records, EFCC publications, court documents and dated procurement records. Avoid reproducing allegations without a primary record and a clear status such as allegation, charge, judgment or dismissal.

#### Legal Considerations

- The Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 is administered by the Nigeria Data Protection Commission; consult current NDPC guidance before processing personal data.
- Defamation, privacy, cybercrime and access-control rules may apply even when information is visible online.
- This section is a research guide, not legal advice.
